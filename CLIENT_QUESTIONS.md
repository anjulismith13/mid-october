# Client Questions

1. Does the export include every employee for every week, including weeks when they worked zero hours? This determines whether a missing employee-week should be treated as explicitly zero hours or as missing data, perhaps an employee working on a different project rather than assigned to this one and given no work. It affects weekly and daily averages, total employee coverage, and anomaly detection. 

2. Given the nature of this project and its employees, is it more valuable to compare unusual rates against that employee’s own history or against rates for people in the same trade and level?

3. Can an employee have multiple records for the same week—for example, split trades, corrections, or separate work locations?

4. Does the benefits rate apply to standard and overtime hours alike?

5. Are there multi-national projects where rates and totals may be in different currencies?
